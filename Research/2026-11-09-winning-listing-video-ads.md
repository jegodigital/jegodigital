## Method
- Meta Ad Library, active video ads only. US = housing category ("just listed", "home tour"). MX = "departamentos", "preventa", "recorrido", "Cancun", "inmobiliaria", sorted by total impressions where Meta allows it (MX yes, US housing no).
- About 300 ads reviewed. "Winning" proxy = still live after many weeks or months + creative duplicated or remade + (MX) top impressions rank.
- Caveat: Meta hides spend and results for these ads. These are signals, not proven ROI.

## Rules for every client video
1. Location, price or a named buyer on screen by second 3.
2. Vertical 9:16, captions burned in, works with sound off.
3. Phone-native beats polished intros (3 of 5 shot on a phone). No logo intros.
4. First copy line = "Desde $X MDP" + zone.
5. Show proof: construction progress, real address, house number.
6. One CTA, into WhatsApp (Babilion qualifies).
7. Two cuts per listing: a 30-60s ad + a long tour for retargeting and Reels.
8. Launch 2 angles per listing; keep the one still getting cheap leads after 2 weeks.
9. Only use client-verifiable numbers (returns, rent, % sold).
